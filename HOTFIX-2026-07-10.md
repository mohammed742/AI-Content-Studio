## What was wrong

We picked models by checking Muapi's catalog list. Turns out being in the list doesn't mean the model actually works — some return 404 when you try to generate. The only reliable check is to actually call the model. These are confirmed dead:

- `flux-schnell`, `hidream-i1-full`, `hidream-i1-fast`, `flux-dev` (image models)
- `mmaudio-v2-text-to-audio` (the cheap voiceover model)
- `bytedance-seedream-v4-edit` (the before/after enhancement model)

The fix is already in the shared plan files. You just need to check your own repo and your own Linear board.

## The lesson (worth remembering)

A model can be listed in an API's catalog, have a price, and still not work. Before trusting any external model or endpoint, actually call it once. "It's in the docs" is not the same as "it works."
